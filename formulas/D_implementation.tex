\subsection{What the supplied checks establish}
The preserved suites cover the following distinct tasks:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}lX@{}}\toprule
Suite & Certificate scope\\\midrule
\texttt{checks} & Universal polynomial identities for the finite kernels; exact local source/product and gauge tests; parity source map; commutator cycle; normalization restrictions.\\[2pt]
\texttt{checks\_v04} & The four source-deformation classes, complete finite parity calibration, open-charge bridge, representative changes and the adopted calibrated wrapper.\\[2pt]
\texttt{checks\_v05} & Exact Bockstein matrices, modular linear solver, finite-dyadic source/pair quotient computations, signed product period and circle carries.\\[2pt]
\texttt{checks\_v06} & Complete physical $C_2$ roots and incoming quotients, structured-manifold characteristic checks, and Chern-character polynomial identities.\\[2pt]
\texttt{tests} & Editorial regression: current public API, integral-Cartan consolidation, cubic-polarizer regrouping, calibrated convention selection, and unchanged coefficient tables.\\\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}
An exact polynomial identity is different from a finite set of sampled simplex inputs. Both are valuable, but their scopes are recorded separately. Similarly, an exact algebraic period on a universal pair cycle is not automatically a physical bordism evaluation; the structured $\mathbb{RP}^5$ test is justified geometrically in Section~\ref{subsec:decisive-bordism}. The topology used to identify the universal cohomology model is proved in the text rather than inferred from the rank program.

\subsection{Connection to the AHSS repository}
The public AHSS repository supplies the notational pattern: degree-indexed decoration fields, actual output decorations, additive hatted obstructions and twisters, fixed higher-cup/MS-word conventions, and paired source/product transport. Its existing parity formula registry is not an implementation of the present insulator theory. This package supplies its own registry and a dictionary to the preserved exact evaluators. No unsupported claim of a repository merge, a new GAP backend, or a fresh space-group computation is made.
