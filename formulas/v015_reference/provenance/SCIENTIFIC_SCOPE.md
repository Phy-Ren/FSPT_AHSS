# Scope of the claims in v0.15

## New proofs, not inherited claims

**Finite additional symmetry.** Under the stated physical AHSS assumptions, positive filtration is finite and the invariant uniform coefficient survives rationally. Its allowed image is a finite-index free sublattice. The full Abelian group splits abstractly over that lattice. This does not identify the primitive response lattice and does not extend to infinite crystalline quotients. It also does not prove a candidate positive-filtration model is the physical one.

**Recognition.** Actual invertible phases with a faithful structured-bordism evaluation image saturating an independent order bound determine the physical group and its exact relation lattice. No universal local change of cochain variables is needed for this conclusion. A finite set of examples does not establish a universal theory for all groups.

**Even-J product.** Domain: rho1=0, n0I=0, n2=-8 e2, n4 even. The bosonic interpretation requires n0J even. The explicit K4 identity includes the induced charge dH3 and its phase 2 omega_checked_R H3. It is not a comparison for all electronic gauge transformations or arbitrary intrinsic inputs.

**All-N physical cyclic target.** Uses actual Weyl anomaly theories, a primitive generator change, the augmentation relations, the integral -x c^2 response, a determinant lower bound and the independent Spin-c AHSS upper bound. The Smith formula is proved analytically. The candidate-to-physical comparison is asserted at the thirteen N values actually calculated, not all N by interpolation.

## Inherited results preserved with their hypotheses

All adopted local obstruction, stacking and gauge formulas are unchanged. The original fixed-natural-model coherence proof and its exact finite certificates are retained. Reduced-model normalization exhaustion, neutral construction, signed primary convention, physical roots and prior calibration statements retain their original domains. No new universal physical theorem is inferred merely from passing regression tests.

## What the new abstract splitting removes from the classification problem

After physical positive filtration F1 has been identified for a finite quotient, intrinsic-only modifications can change the allowed embedded response lattice and the chosen local section, but cannot change the abstract torsion group or free rank. Consequently those missing uniform-response comparisons need not block an **abstract** classification in an already calibrated case.

## What remains material

For a general additional symmetry, the physical identification of the positive-filtration model remains a real requirement. The general terminal parity R5/K4 and exhaustive opened-model deformation-space comparison have not been proved here. The nonzero uniform-IQH two-dimensional convention likewise retains its qualification. In the two-Weyl-pair (C2)^4 example, 2J is physically realized; exclusion or realization of 2I is not newly settled in this release. None of these are silently changed to zero or declared irrelevant to response-labelled physics.

The manuscript is a technically complete preprint package for its stated results, not a certificate that every possible electronic phase has been uniquely identified by the operative model.
