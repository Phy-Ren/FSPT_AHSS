# 4+1D internal cyclic groups: zero intrinsic response

Each row is one exact internal electronic symmetry; crystalline spin labels do not apply. E8, CI, charge and the common phase are computed with complete products and gauges. The intrinsic response pair (n0I,n0J) is fixed to zero. Reference answers are applied after calculation. Physical derivations and their exact scope are recorded in the cited sources and accepted evidence.
