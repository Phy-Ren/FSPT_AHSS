# 4+1D internal cyclic groups: all intrinsic responses

Each row is one exact internal electronic symmetry; crystalline spin labels do not apply. E8, CI, charge and the common phase are computed with complete products and gauges. The two symmetry-independent intrinsic roots I,J supply an explicit canonical Z^2 section for this direct-product symmetry. The general opened intrinsic solver is not inferred. Reference answers are applied after calculation. Physical derivations and their exact scope are recorded in the cited sources and accepted evidence.
