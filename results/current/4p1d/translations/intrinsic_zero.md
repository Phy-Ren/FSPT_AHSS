# 4+1D translations: zero intrinsic response

Each row specifies the free translation rank in four spatial dimensions. Only rank four is a full space-group translation lattice; lower ranks impose fewer translations. There are no rotations and hence no distinct spin lifts. The complete finite simplicial torus retains E8, CI, charge, gauges and the actual stacking correction. The intrinsic response pair (n0I,n0J) is fixed to zero. Reference answers are applied after calculation. Physical derivations and their exact scope are recorded in the cited sources and accepted evidence.
