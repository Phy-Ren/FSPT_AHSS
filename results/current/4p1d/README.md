# 4+1D internal insulator examples

The cyclic pilot computes **C1 through C6** for unitary internal symmetry `U(1)^f x C_N`, with `s=rho=omega=0`. Internal backgrounds have no crystalline spin labels.

- [Six cyclic groups](internal_cyclic.md) · [CSV](internal_cyclic.csv) · [JSON](internal_cyclic.json).

The four data are `(e2,n2,n4,nu5)`. E8, CI, charge, and the single shared phase enter the actual root-relation presentation; the final group includes their stacking extensions. The 3+1D E8 factorization is not assumed here.

These are completed examples of the original four-data model. A general global 4+1D classifier and the intrinsic `r0` response are outside this collection. Independent full interacting electronic comparisons remain unverified.
