# Calculation provenance and scope

The catalogue selects one accepted calculation per exact input and response sector. It uses the 64 completed E8-inclusive point-group records and 44 completed 2+1D records from the October 15 campaign. The remaining 64 2+1D sector entries use the exhaustive October 13 campaign. For an entry without a separate n0=0 rerun, its value is the saved kernel of the actual IQH response homomorphism, not an inferred removal of a free factor. Dates, input/source hashes, immutable record locations and exact selectors are retained in [index.json](index.json); consumed file hashes are in [manifest.json](manifest.json).

The final filtration columns are e_p, n_p, n_(p+2), nu_(p+3), with p=d−2. They already include the computed incoming identifications. Their direct product is not assumed to equal the final stacking group. The 2+1D tables impose e0=0; the 3+1D tables include independently computed H1(G,Z_s) and the selected E8 section. The remaining identification of the CI model with the complete physical kernel is not certified by a matching abstract group. Intrinsic higher-response sectors are outside these tables.

Every literature version has its own column. The 3+1D point-group reference columns use full interacting source groups, without removing E8 factors. The 2+1D references are restricted to the stated n0/e0 sector. Wang–Qi–Fang–Cheng–Gu columns are charge and phase filtration layers, not stacking groups. Unavailable source values remain UNVERIFIED; an inapplicable source is N/A. No free-band classification or band-generated subgroup is used as a complete interacting benchmark.

The [source ledger](../../docs/reference_ledger_20261014.md), [foundation review](../../docs/foundations/README.md), and immutable [October 15 campaign](../four_data_campaign_20261015/README.md) retain detailed calibration and validation evidence. Printed Debray D2/D6 values remain unchanged, with their specific proof-grading audit linked in the tables. Published tables are comparisons, never solver inputs.

Additional accepted collections are listed explicitly in [collections.json](collections.json). The builder never scans unfinished runs. Each expansion is accepted by `scripts/collect_expansion.py`, which verifies every selected run, exact CI parent artifacts, source/input hashes, native completion records, and independent integer presentations. Space-group Song–Xiong–Huang comparisons concern only the neutral E8 coefficient; full electronic references remain unverified. 4+1D cyclic filtration entries are derived from the actual root-relation matrix by saturated integer elimination, without treating equal layer orders as the stacking answer.

- [Independent cyclic complete-root and native-boundary audit](../expansion_20261015/4p1d/cyclic_root_audit.json): 6 presentations and 20 native phase boundaries verified. Its result/source hashes are checked against the accepted collection.

Rebuild with `python3 scripts/build_current_tables.py`; verify deterministic output with `python3 scripts/build_current_tables.py --check`. Historical artifacts are read-only.
