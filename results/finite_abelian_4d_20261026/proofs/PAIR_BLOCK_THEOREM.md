# Complete split two-factor 2-primary block

Derivation, 2026-10-26.
The proof uses standard primary cohomology operations, naturality, and three exact finite base computations already in the verified catalog.
No extrapolation of a numerical pattern in the exponents is needed.

## Statement

Let m=2^a <= n=2^b, with a,b>=1, and let

    K(m,n) = reduced_Omega_5^Spin(BC_m smash BC_n).

Then

    K(2,2) = 0;
    K(2,4) = C_2;
    K(2,2^b) = C_4,                       b>=3;
    K(2^a,2^a) = C_{2^{a-1}} x C_{2^a},  a>=2;
    K(2^a,2^b) = C_{2^{a-1}} x C_{2^{a+1}}, 2<=a<b.

As throughout the project, the Pontryagin dual has the same invariant factors.

## Cohomology conventions

Use x_i in H^1(BC_{N_i},F_2), and the integral generator Y_i in H^2(BC_{N_i},Z).
Write y_i for its mod-2 reduction.
Then beta(x_i)=(N_i/2)Y_i and x_i^2=(N_i/2 mod2)y_i.
Here beta is the integral Bockstein for Z --2--> Z -> F_2.
For N_i=2 this is the polynomial degree-one cohomology algebra; for 4|N_i it is exterior(x_i) tensor polynomial(y_i).

The two bosonic mixed classes b_1,b_2 are defined through their integral Bocksteins:

    beta_U1(b_1)=Y_1^2 Y_2,
    beta_U1(b_2)=Y_1 Y_2^2.

Before fermionic identifications, each has order m.
The integral cohomology Bockstein identifies H^5(U1) with H^6(Z) for these finite groups.

## 1. Lower layers and the first bosonic quotient

There is no p+ip layer on this reduced smash support.
The Majorana classes in degree three are x_1 y_2 and y_1 x_2.
Sq^2 sends them to x_1 y_2^2 and y_1^2 x_2, respectively, so its kernel is zero.
There is therefore no surviving Majorana decoration in this block.

The complex-fermion obstruction kernel in degree four is generated by y_1 y_2.
For (m,n)=(2,2), this is the incoming image Sq^2(x_1 x_2), so the final complex-fermion layer is zero.
For every other pair the final complex-fermion layer is C_2, with generator denoted f when lifted to the complete phase.

The incoming complex-fermion identifications satisfy

    beta Sq^2(x_1 y_2) = (m/2)Y_1 Y_2^2,
    beta Sq^2(y_1 x_2) = (n/2)Y_1^2 Y_2.

Thus the preliminary bosonic quotient has the presentation

    m b_1 = m b_2 = 0,
    (n/2)b_1 = (m/2)b_2 = 0.

It is C_{m/2}^2 when a=b, and C_m x C_{m/2} when a<b.
For (2,2) it is zero, and there is no admissible incoming Majorana source because Sq^2(x_1x_2) is nonzero.

The complex-fermion kernel statement can also be checked directly in the tensor resolution.
The possible degree-four terms are of multidegrees (1,3),(2,2),(3,1).
The integral-lift obstruction for Sq^2 kills the first and last and preserves (2,2).
The exact Smith-decomposition implementation is `../low_operations.py`.

## 2. The incoming Majorana secondary obstruction

For all pairs other than (2,2), the sole possible source is x_1x_2 in H^2(F_2).
The source and a chosen trivialization of Sq^2(x_1x_2) pull back from C_2 x C_4 under the quotient map

    C_m x C_n -> C_2 x C_4.

This includes the equal-order case m=n>=4.
The exact base computation for (2,4) gives the nonzero secondary obstruction b_1 in its preliminary bosonic quotient C_2.
Its integral Bockstein is Y_1^2Y_2.
Under the quotient map, the integral generators pull back by

    Y_1(base) -> (m/2)Y_1,
    Y_2(base) -> (n/4)Y_2.

Therefore the secondary obstruction pulls back to

    (m^2 n/16)b_1.

This is nonzero only when (m,n)=(2,4).
For a=b>=2 its coefficient is divisible by m/2.
For a<b with (a,b)!=(1,2), it is divisible by m.
It therefore vanishes in the preliminary bosonic quotient in every other case.
The ambiguity in the secondary operation lies in the already divided-out incoming complex-fermion image.

Hence the final bosonic group is zero for (2,4); otherwise it is the preliminary quotient above.
This proves the secondary-obstruction dependence on arbitrary exponents from a single finite certificate and naturality.

## 3. Stacking extensions from finite restrictions

For a>=2, the final bosonic group has generators b_1,b_2 of orders m/2,m/2 if a=b, and m,m/2 if a<b.
The extension by the complex-fermion C_2 is determined by the class of 2f in B/2B.

Restrict along the coordinate inclusions

    C_4 x C_4 -> C_m x C_n.

Each integral Y_i restricts to the primitive integral generator, and y_1y_2 restricts to the nonzero complex-fermion class.
The bosonic generators b_1,b_2 consequently restrict to the corresponding two order-two generators of the (4,4) block.
The verified (4,4) block is C_2 x C_4, so the extension there is nonzero.
Exchange of the two equal C_4 factors interchanges b_1 and b_2 while preserving the complex-fermion quotient generator.
Since 2B=0 in this base block, this forces

    2f = b_1+b_2        in the (4,4) base block.

Naturality of restriction forces both coefficients of 2f in B/2B to be odd for all a,b>=2.
Odd changes of generators normalize the general extension to the same relation 2f=b_1+b_2.
Its Smith form gives the two claimed formulas for a>=2.

For a=1 and b>=3, restrict instead to C_2 x C_8.
The exact base block is C_4, with bosonic subgroup C_2 and complex-fermion quotient C_2.
The bosonic and complex-fermion generators restrict nontrivially, so the general extension is again nontrivial.
It gives C_4 independently of b>=3.

Finally (2,4) has no final bosonic subgroup and one complex-fermion class, so its group is C_2.
The (2,2) block is zero by step 1.

## Finite certificates required by the proof

Only the following fixed blocks are needed:

1. (2,4): preliminary bosonic C_2 is killed by the incoming Majorana secondary obstruction; final CF=C_2 and final group C_2.
2. (4,4): final bosonic C_2^2, CF=C_2, final group C_2 x C_4.
3. (2,8): final bosonic C_2, CF=C_2, final group C_4.

These follow from the exact catalog by removing singleton direct summands.
The archived groups and calculation receipts are included in this package.
The extraction is independently recorded in `../data/archived_51.json`.
The general reduction uses these three finite certified base calculations; no exponent-pattern extrapolation is used.

## Independent check and geometric interpretation

The extension relation also has the expected continuous-symmetry origin: on a closed spin six-manifold, Wu's formula makes

    integral (Y_1^2Y_2+Y_1Y_2^2)

even.
The half-integral mixed spin Chern-Simons polynomial therefore refines the sum of the two bosonic cubic responses.
The finite-restriction argument above avoids assuming that this continuous response alone exhausts all finite-symmetry anomalies.
