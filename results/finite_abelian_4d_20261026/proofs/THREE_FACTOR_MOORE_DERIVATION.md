# Complete split three-factor 2-primary block

Derivation, 2026-10-26.
This proof combines exact primary-operation algebra, a constructive complex-fermion doubling relation, and the low stable cells of cyclic classifying spaces.
It does not extrapolate a table of finite examples.

Let N_1=2^a <= N_2=2^b <= N_3=2^c and g=N_1.
The full reduced degree-five spin-bordism group of the threefold smash is

    a=b=c=1:       C_2^3;
    a=b=1<c:       C_2^4;
    a=1,b=2:       C_4 x C_2^3;
    a=1,b>=3:      C_8 x C_2^3;
    2<=a=b:        C_2 x C_g^4;
    2<=a<b:        C_2 x C_{2g} x C_g^3.

The Pontryagin-dual stacking group has the same invariant factors.

## 1. Exact primary-operation calculation

Write x_i for the degree-one mod-2 class and Y_i for the integral degree-two class, with y_i=rho_2(Y_i).
As usual x_i^2=epsilon_i y_i, epsilon_i=1 for N_i=2 and zero otherwise.

The bosonic group is C_g^4 before identifications.
The only incoming complex-fermion source is x_1x_2x_3 in degree three.
Its image is detected by beta Sq^2:

    beta Sq^2(x_1x_2x_3)
      = sum_{cyclic i,j,k} epsilon_i epsilon_j (N_k/2)Y_1Y_2Y_3.

It is nonzero precisely when all N_i=2.
Thus the final bosonic subgroup is C_2^3 in that case, and C_g^4 otherwise.
There is no incoming Majorana identification because reduced H^2 of this smash is zero, and no incoming p+ip identification because H^1(Z)=0.

The Majorana first-obstruction kernel is generated by

    a_3=x_1x_2x_3

when fewer than two factors have order two, and is zero otherwise.
This follows directly from Sq^2(a_3)=sum epsilon_i epsilon_j y_i y_j x_k.

The complex-fermion obstruction kernel is one-dimensional exactly when the smallest N_i is unique.
Its generator is n_4=y_1x_2x_3.
Otherwise the kernel is zero.
No incoming term changes this kernel.

Here is an integral-resolution verification of the last assertion.
The candidates are y_i x_jx_k, and the relevant part of their Sq^2 image has multidegree (4,1,1), with the 4 in position i.
Different positions lie in different integral Koszul blocks and cannot cancel.
An integral lift of that image exists exactly when both N_j/N_i and N_k/N_i are even integers.
This is precisely the unique-minimum condition.
The possible extra term of multidegree (2,2,2) already has an integral lift and does not alter this obstruction.
The exact matrix implementation `../low_operations.py` verifies these statements directly by Smith decomposition.

## 2. The complex-fermion doubling relation

Assume the minimum g=N_1 is unique.
Use integral cyclic-resolution cochains X_i,Y_i with dX_i=N_iY_i and dY_i=0.
Set

    L_6 = (1/g)d(X_1Y_1X_2X_3)
        = Y_1^2X_2X_3
          -(N_2/g)X_1Y_1Y_2X_3
          +(N_3/g)X_1Y_1X_2Y_3.

Both ratios are even, so rho_2(L_6)=Sq^2(n_4) in cohomology.
A representative of the complex-fermion obstruction can therefore be chosen as L_6/2, with phase primitive

    nu_5 = X_1Y_1X_2X_3/(2g).

Its double is the primitive order-g bosonic class

    b = X_1Y_1X_2X_3/g.

The integral class L_6 is primitive in its Koszul block, so b has order exactly g.
The self-stacking twister contributes Sq^1(n_4)/2, which is cohomologically zero because N_2,N_3 are divisible by four.
Changing to the displayed obstruction representative uses a half-valued coboundary correction; its double vanishes and does not change this relation.
Consequently the lifted complex-fermion phase f satisfies

    2f=b.

The complete complex-fermion plus bosonic subgroup is therefore C_{2g} x C_g^3.

## 3. Low-cell presentation

Let M_N be the stable Moore spectrum, the cofiber of multiplication by N on S^0.
The reduced 3-skeleton of BC_N stably splits as

    Sigma M_N wedge S^3.

The degree-three attaching map is zero because its only possible stable component is detected by Sq^2 on H^1, which vanishes by instability.
The degree-four cell attaches to this wedge by a pair

    (u_N,N),       u_N in pi_2(M_N),       q u_N=eta.

The latter equality is detected by Sq^2(y)=y^2.
Only the following standard low Moore facts are needed:

    pi_0(M_N)=C_N,   pi_1(M_N)=C_2;
    pi_2(M_2)=C_4, with 2u_2=i eta^2;
    pi_2(M_N)=C_2^2 for 4|N;
    N id_(M_N)=(N/2 mod2)i eta q.

The unit S -> MSpin is an isomorphism in stems 0,1,2, so these stable groups compute the required low relative spin-bordism groups.

Put T=M_{N_1} smash M_{N_2} smash M_{N_3}.
The Moore part contributes pi_2(T) to dimension five.
The three S^3 summands give generators e_i of order gcd(N_j,N_k), with {i,j,k}={1,2,3}.
The three degree-four cells impose exactly

    N_i e_i + image(u_{N_i}) = 0.

No other cells can affect degree five.
In particular, degree-five cells in a single BC_N first contribute to dimension seven after smashing with the other two reduced factors.
The stable 3-skeleton splitting makes the relations on e_i from the other factors exactly N_j e_i=N_k e_i=0; there is no omitted coupling to T in those relations.

## 4. All cyclic orders at least four

Suppose g>=4.
The Moore identity makes multiplication by every other N_i null on M_g.
Thus

    T = M_g wedge 2 Sigma M_g wedge Sigma^2 M_g,
    pi_2(T)=C_g x C_2^4.

If the minimum is repeated, all three e_i have order g.
Before the three remaining attachments, the group has order 16g^4.
Each attachment has order at most two, so the quotient has order at least 2g^4.
The primary-operation calculation gives the upper bound 2g^4: bosonic order g^4, no complex fermion, and at most one Majorana C_2.
The bounds agree, proving that the Majorana class survives its secondary obstruction.
Every initial generator has order dividing g, so the quotient exponent divides g.
A nontrivial extension of C_2 by C_g^4 would have exponent 2g.
The extension must therefore split, giving C_2 x C_g^4.

If the minimum is unique, write h=N_2.
The generator e_1 has order h, while e_2,e_3 have order g.
The two relations from i=2,3 have order at most two.
The remaining relation g e_1+u_{N_1} has order at most h/g because h/g is even and u_{N_1} has order two.
The final order is consequently at least

    (16g)(h)(g^2) / (4h/g) = 4g^4.

The primary-operation upper bound is exactly 4g^4, so the Majorana class again survives.
The group is generated by one element e_1 of order at most 2g and other elements of order at most g.
It can therefore contain at most one invariant factor of order 2g and none of order 4g.
Section 2 already supplies one C_{2g} factor in the complex-fermion plus bosonic subgroup.
Any nontrivial remaining Majorana extension would produce either an order-4g factor or a second order-2g factor.
Both are excluded, so this extension splits.
The full group is C_2 x C_{2g} x C_g^3.

## 5. Exactly one cyclic factor of order two

Now let N_1=2 and N_2=h>=4.
The other Moore multiplications are divisible by four and hence null on M_2.
Therefore

    pi_2(T)=C_4 x C_2^3.

If h=4, the relation 2e_1+u_2=0 has twice-relation

    4e_1+2u_2=i eta^2=0.

Here 4e_1=0 already follows from the second factor.
The bottom eta^2 class is precisely the Majorana associated-graded generator, so it is killed.
Section 2 then gives the complete group C_4 x C_2^3.

If h>=8, the initial order is (32)(h)(2^2)=128h.
The two attachments from the higher-order factors have order at most two.
The remaining relation 2e_1+u_2 has order at most h/2, since h/2 is divisible by four.
The final order is therefore at least 64.
The primary-operation upper bound is also 64: bosonic order16, one complex-fermion C_2, and at most one Majorana C_2.
Thus the Majorana survives.
Its self-stacking complex-fermion class is

    Sq^1(x_1x_2x_3)=y_1x_2x_3,

the nontrivial complex-fermion generator.
Section 2 gives that generator order four, with nontrivial bosonic double.
Adding any order-two bosonic phase cannot change its order four.
The Majorana lift therefore has order eight, and the complete group is C_8 x C_2^3.

## 6. At least two factors of order two

The Majorana first obstruction is already nonzero, and the complex-fermion layer is zero.
There is no extension to resolve.
The bosonic calculation in section 1 gives C_2^3 for three order-two factors and C_2^4 for exactly two.
