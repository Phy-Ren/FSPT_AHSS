# Surviving decoration layers for split finite Abelian symmetry

Derived from outgoing obstructions and incoming identifications, 2026-10-26.
These are the associated-graded groups, before resolving stacking extensions.
They are not inferred from the invariant factors of the final stacking group.
The full split group is the direct product of its contributions from subsets of cyclic factors, as proved by the stable splitting.
The same decomposition respects the decoration filtration.

The four layers in 4+1D are:

    p+ip:       H^2(G,Z),
    Majorana:   H^3(G,Z2),
    complex:    H^4(G,Z2),
    bosonic:    H^5(G,U1).

On the reduced support of k cyclic factors, use the tensor cyclic resolution with

    dx_i=N_i y_i,   dy_i=0,
    [x_i]_2^2=[N_i/2]_2 [y_i]_2,
    beta[x_i]_2=(N_i/2)y_i.

Here beta is the integral Bockstein.
For the complex-fermion obstruction, the cohomological test is beta Sq^2, not Sq^2 alone.
An Sq^2 image with an integral cocycle lift has zero U1 obstruction.

## Complete layer rules

### Odd-primary factors

For a single cyclic factor of order N=p^a with odd p:

    p+ip = Z_N;  Majorana = 0;  complex = 0;  bosonic = Z_N.

For mixed supports, only the bosonic layer survives.
Its groups for k=2,3,4,5 are respectively

    Z_g^2, Z_g^4, Z_g^3, Z_g,

where g is the smallest cyclic order.
The order-three enhancement of the full cyclic group is a stacking extension between its p+ip and bosonic layers.
It does not alter these two layer groups.

## Outgoing obstructions and incoming quotients

### 1. One cyclic factor

For odd N there is no positive-degree mod-two cohomology.
Both fermionic mod-two layers and every obstruction through those layers vanish.
The integer class survives because its final obstruction target H^6(Z_N,U1) is zero.
There is no incoming integer identification because H^1(Z_N,Z)=0.
Thus the integer and bosonic layers are both Z_N.

For even N, the first p+ip obstruction sends the primitive integer generator y to [y]_2^2.
Its kernel is 2Z_N, of order N/2.
The next obstruction has zero target after quotient by the Majorana primary image:

    Sq^2([xy]_2)=[xy^2]_2

spans H^5(Z_N,Z2).
This means that an allowed choice of Majorana decoration cancels any secondary p+ip class.
It does not assert that the secondary obstruction vanishes as a bare cochain.
The terminal p+ip obstruction target is again H^6(Z_N,U1)=0.

The Majorana class [xy]_2 is removed by its nonzero Sq^2 image.
The complex-fermion class [y^2]_2 passes its outgoing obstruction, but is the incoming image Sq^2[y]_2.
Consequently the final complex-fermion layer is zero.

The incoming complex-fermion phase identification is

    beta Sq^2[xy]_2 = (N/2)y^3.

It removes the order-two subgroup of the original bosonic Z_N.
There is no incoming Majorana secondary identification: its possible source [y]_2 fails the first obstruction Sq^2[y]_2=[y^2]_2.
Thus the final bosonic quotient is Z_(N/2).

### 2. Two cyclic factors

Reduced H^2(Z) and H^1(Z) vanish, so there is neither a p+ip layer nor an incoming p+ip identification.
The two degree-three Majorana classes [x1 y2]_2 and [y1 x2]_2 have independent nonzero Sq^2 images.
The outgoing Majorana kernel is therefore zero.

The kernel of beta Sq^2 on the complex-fermion layer is generated by [y1 y2]_2.
The incoming Majorana primary image kills it only for (m,n)=(2,2), since

    Sq^2[x1 x2]_2 = [m/2]_2[n/2]_2[y1 y2]_2.

The two original bosonic factors have generators C1,C2 of order m.
Incoming complex-fermion identifications impose

    (n/2)C1=0,  (m/2)C2=0.

This leaves Z_(m/2)^2 when m=n, and Z_m×Z_(m/2) when m<n.
The incoming Majorana secondary operation on [x1 x2]_2 removes one further class exactly for (m,n)=(2,4).
Its uniform pullback coefficient is m^2 n/16, as proved in PAIR_BLOCK_THEOREM.md.
These outgoing and incoming calculations give every pair row of the table.

### 3. Three cyclic factors

There is no p+ip layer or integer identification.
There is also no degree-two Majorana source, so neither its primary nor its secondary incoming identification affects this support.

The only Majorana candidate is [x1 x2 x3]_2.
Its first obstruction is nonzero exactly when at least two cyclic factors have order two.
For a unique order-two minimum, its secondary obstruction is nonzero exactly when the second smallest order is four.
It vanishes in the outgoing quotient when the second smallest order is at least eight.
When all orders are at least four, the secondary obstruction also vanishes.
These secondary statements follow from the low-cell Moore-spectrum order bounds in THREE_FACTOR_MOORE_DERIVATION.md; the bounds equal the decoration-layer upper bounds.
They do not rely on guessing a filtration from the final abstract group.

The outgoing complex-fermion kernel is generated by [y1 x2 x3]_2 exactly when N1 is uniquely smallest.
To see sufficiency, the integral cocycle

    (1/N1)d(x1 y1 x2 x3)

lifts its Sq^2 image precisely when N2/N1 and N3/N1 are even.
Necessity follows from the independent integral multidegree components of the three candidate obstruction terms.
There is no incoming identification on this layer.

The original bosonic group is Z_g^4.
Its only possible incoming complex-fermion source is [x1 x2 x3]_2.
The class beta Sq^2 of this source is nonzero only when all three orders are two.
In that case it removes one Z_2 and leaves Z_2^3.
Otherwise it leaves Z_g^4 unchanged.

### 4. Four cyclic factors

There are no integer or Majorana decorations and no incoming identifications in the relevant degrees.
The complex-fermion candidate is [x1 x2 x3 x4]_2.
If zero or one factor has order two, its Sq^2 image is zero.
If exactly two factors have order two, label them 1 and 2.
The Sq^2 image is [y1 y2 x3 x4]_2, with an explicit integral lift

    (1/2)d(x1 y2 x3 x4)
      = y1 y2 x3 x4
        -(N3/2)x1 y2 y3 x4
        +(N4/2)x1 y2 x3 y4.

The last two coefficients are even, so beta Sq^2 vanishes.
Thus the complex-fermion Z_2 survives for at most two order-two factors.
If at least three factors have order two, the mod-two reduction

    rho_2 beta Sq^2 = Sq^1 Sq^2 = Sq^3

is nonzero on this candidate.
The complex-fermion class is therefore obstructed.
The bosonic group Z_g^3 receives no incoming identification and remains unchanged.

### 5. Five cyclic factors

Connectivity removes all lower layers and incoming sources.
Only the bosonic group Z_g remains.

## Elementary Abelian consequence

For Gf=Z2f×Z2^r, every singleton and pair support vanishes.
Every triple contributes only bosonic Z2^3, every quadruple only bosonic Z2^3, and every quintuple only bosonic Z2.
The complete associated-graded group is therefore entirely bosonic:

    Z2^[3 binom(r,3) + 3 binom(r,4) + binom(r,5)].

There is no fermionic stacking extension left to resolve.
This verifies the main-text boson-only statement for every rank.
