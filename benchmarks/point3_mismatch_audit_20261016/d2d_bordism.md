# Independent interacting bordism calculation for D2d, both spins

2026-10-16. This is a new calculation, not a transcription of a printed D2d bordism answer. No classifier result or expected reference group was an input to the Ext computation. Production files were not changed.

## Result

For physical charge-preserving D2d symmetry in three spatial dimensions, the independent twisted Spin^c computation gives:

### Spinless

The relevant Thom spectrum has ku4=Z2³ and ku0=Z2. Its complete Spin^c / interacting phase group is **Z2⁴**.

### Spin-half

The relevant Thom spectrum has ku4=Z8×Z2² and ku0=Z2. Its complete Spin^c / interacting phase group is **Z8×Z2³**.

These are complete groups within the shared interacting crystalline/bordism ansatz, with no unresolved Adams differentials or additive extensions in degree four. They include the full low-degree Spin^c coefficient spectrum and do not remove an E8 root. Their agreement with the project's current groups is an output comparison.

## Symmetry identification from the catalogue and primary formulas

Write D8 = <r,s | r4=s2=1, srs=r−1>. In the actual D2d catalogue, r is element 2, a quarter-turn in the xy plane combined with z inversion; s is element 5, a proper π rotation about x. Let u(r)=1,u(s)=0 and v(r)=0,v(s)=1. The representation splits exactly as

    V = W_xy ⊕ line_(u+v),   w1(W_xy)=v,   w2(W_xy)=w.

The independent exact matrix audit checks all 64 products, the plane determinant and z-line characters for all eight elements, both catalogue signs s=rho=u, the spinless Pin-minus cocycle, and the zero effective extension for spin-half.

The primary mathematical input is Debray [arXiv:2102.02941v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.02941v3), Eq. (4.15a,b) and Proposition 4.16 (p.36; `dihedral.tex:52–70`), with x=v,y=u, and Lemma 4.29 (p.39):

    H*(BD8,F2)=F2[u,v,w]/(u²+uv),  |u|=|v|=1, |w|=2,
    Sq1(w)=vw,  Sq2(w)=w².

The square on w also follows directly from the rank-two Wu formula. For a=u,b=u+v, the ring becomes F2[a,b,w]/(ab).

The class-A shearing theorem, Theorem 2.24 (p.19), gives the rank-zero Thom twists

    X0 = (BD8)^(3−V),
    Xhalf = (BD8)^(det(V)−1).

Here V is not Pin^c: reduction of βw2(V) is vw≠0. Its characteristic classes yield

    w1(X0)=a,    w2(X0)=w+(a+b)²;
    w1(Xhalf)=a, w2(Xhalf)=0.

Thus the computation uses the actual spatial representation, not the planar D4 representation merely because the abstract groups agree.

## Independent Koszul computation

With Thom generator U, write S1,S2 for the Thom Steenrod actions:

    S1(f)=Sq1(f)+a f,
    S2(f)=Sq2(f)+a Sq1(f)+w2(X)f,
    Q0=S1,  Q1=S1 S2+S2 S1.

The dual Koszul complex computing Ext over E(1)=Λ(Q0,Q1) has

    C^(s,t)=⊕_(j=0)^s M_(t−s−2j)*,
    d=h0 Q0*+v1 Q1*.

`d2d_ext_audit.py` constructs the polynomial action and this complex directly over F2. It verifies the exterior-algebra identities, d²=0, and the h0 chain-map identity. The low-stem groups are computed through filtration twelve. Above filtration four all cell ranges and matrices affecting stems zero through five stabilize, because M has no negative-degree part, so the displayed vanishing is not an arbitrary finite cutoff.

The relevant E2 entries, listed by increasing Adams filtration, are:

For **spinless**, stems three, four, and five are respectively `(1,0,…)`, `(2,1,0,…)`, and `(2,0,…)`; the stem-four h0 ranks are zero.

For **spin-half**, those stems are respectively `(2,1,0,…)`, `(3,1,1,0,…)`, and `(2,1,1,0,…)`; the stem-four h0 ranks are `(1,1,0,…)`.

For spinless no Adams dr, r≥2, can enter the occupied stem-four filtrations or leave them to stem three. All three classes survive. Multiplication by two raises filtration; its associated h0 action from filtration zero to one is zero and filtration two vanishes. Hence all elements have order two, and ku4(X0)=Z2³.

## The possible spin-half differential is ruled out by an actual retract

There is one apparent candidate: d2 from stem five, filtration zero, to stem four, filtration two. It must be addressed; an E2-page alone is insufficient.

The orientation homomorphism u:D8→C2 admits a section generated by sr, catalogue element 6. This element has order two and u(sr)=1. Since the twist det(V)−1 is pulled back from σ−1 on BC2, the induced Thom-spectrum maps split:

    Y=(BC2)^(σ−1) → Xhalf → Y,   composite=id.

At the polynomial-module level the maps are inclusion of the a-only polynomials and evaluation b=w=0. They preserve the full Thom Steenrod actions. Independently restricting the Koszul calculation to this a-only submodule gives:

    Ext(Y), stem 3 = 0;
    Ext(Y), stem 4 = (1,1,1,0,…), with h0 ranks (1,1);
    Ext(Y), stem 5 = 0.

The Z8 tower therefore survives in Y, and its image in Xhalf survives by the actual spectrum retraction. Thus the candidate incoming d2 is zero. Equivalently, the known Smith equivalence Y≃Σ−1Σ∞BC2 and reduced ku5(BC2)=Z8 give the same check.

The spectrum complement has only two stem-four filtration-zero classes, so it contributes Z2². The actual retract prevents extensions between this complement and the Z8 factor. Therefore ku4(Xhalf)=Z8×Z2².

## Spin^c completion and extraction of the physical group

Both rank-zero Thom spectra are connective. The independently checked 2-local low-degree splitting

    MSpinC ≃ ku ∨ Σ⁴ku  (through degree seven)

therefore gives Ω4^SpinC(X)=ku4(X)⊕ku0(X). Its primary independent references and connectivity justification are recorded in `debray_d2.md`, particularly Buchanan–McKean, AGT 26 (2026), Theorem 3.6 (p.492) and Table 3 (p.538), [DOI:10.2140/agt.2026.26.485](https://doi.org/10.2140/agt.2026.26.485).

For either twist, ku0(X)=H0(BD8,Z_u)=Z2: it is the coinvariant of Z under a nontrivial sign action. D8 is a 2-group, and the sign local system has no rational invariants, so there is no odd torsion or rational free part. In particular Ω5 is torsion. The degree-five Anderson-dual sequence identifies the three-dimensional phase group with the finite Ω4 group, giving the results above.

This independently settles the abstract interacting stacking groups for these two symmetries within the bordism ansatz. It does not construct a root-by-root map to the real-space paper's generators, nor does it turn the previously conditional atomic-orbit quotient into a proved physical bubble deformation. That earlier geometric task is no longer needed to determine these abstract groups.
