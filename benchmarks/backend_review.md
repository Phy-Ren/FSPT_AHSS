# Independent backend review

Reviewed `gap/classification.g`, `gap/cochains.g`, `gap/context.g`, and their
generic native/bar and Smith-form dependencies against the v0.7 source,
product, and gauge equations. This review is not a substitute for the immutable
campaign certificates generated by the runner.

The following mathematical choices were checked:

- Relative 2+1D charge obstruction is the complete integer extension coupling
  plus the open-square term. Integer charge is never replaced by its parity in
  the extension coupling. The source map can be represented linearly on
  cohomology because its polarization is an exact cochain supplied by the
  stacking twister.
- The obstruction kernel is an integer lattice containing the original charge
  relation lattice. Each original charge relation is expressed in that kernel
  basis, stacked with its full phase completion, then removed with the full
  integer charge gauge. This permits nonbinary root orders and nonsplit groups.
- Signed local fields are based at the first vertex of their face. Therefore
  `U1LocalField` should evaluate the inhomogeneous cochain on face increments
  without an additional absolute-vertex sign; the simplex differential and cup
  operations supply the relative transport. The GAP implementation follows
  this convention.
- Native primitives are corrected by the native/bar comparison homotopy.
  This correction is necessary before applying secondary obstruction,
  stacking, or gauge formulas and is retained in the implementation.
- For finite 3D point groups, signed H1 is either zero or Z2. A CI state first
  solves the integral obstruction and then changes its charge completion to
  cancel the terminal class modulo the charge differential image. Its finite
  root is reduced by joint sheet/charge gauge before measuring charge and
  phase coordinates.
- Incoming charge gauges and closed degree-zero CI gauges are inserted as
  relations in the full presentation. Their phase coordinates are retained.
- Wallpaper cohomology uses the full affine group and resolution, and only
  the background is pulled back from finite holonomy. Nonsymmorphic generators
  and the filling class are retained. U(1) cohomology is used only above the
  virtual cohomological dimension, where the torsion Smith construction applies.

A separate local end-to-end calculation tested the unitary 3D D2 point group
with physical spinless fermions. Its raw charge cohomology is Z2 and its raw
phase cohomology is Z2 squared. The incoming CI gauge had charge coordinates
`[1]` and phase coordinates `[0,1]`. The final charge and phase subquotients and
full classification are trivial, agreeing with Table II of
[arXiv:2204.13558](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.13558). This example checks a
nonzero incoming CI image and its phase, rather than a case where the operation
vanishes. The transient audit files are `/tmp/insulator-review-d2.g` and
`/tmp/insulator-review-d2.log`; the run is to be superseded by the immutable
campaign output.

Two verification gaps were reported to the solver author for correction:

1. The synthetic cyclic-context helper originally accepted odd-order sign
   assignments and extension choices without checking that they define a
   character and a twisted cocycle. Catalog backgrounds themselves already
   undergo exhaustive finite-group validation.
2. A successful native solve alone should not serve as a complete global
   certificate. Completed state equations and root/gauge endpoint equations
   should be checked after transfer, with explicit distinction between full
   finite exhaustion and sampled bar evaluations. The worker's disabled
   lower-state check at a degree-matched simplex does not provide such a
   certificate by itself.

The source audit did not identify a missing term in the reviewed relative 2D
root reduction, the reviewed 3D incoming quotient, or the affine wallpaper
construction. This statement is restricted to that review scope and does not
assert completion of every campaign or unimplemented physical coefficient row.
