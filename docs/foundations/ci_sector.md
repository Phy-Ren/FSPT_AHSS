# The current CI/charge/phase model

Status: 2026-10-14. This page describes the implemented model with no
independent neutral E8 decoration. The deferred larger model has its own
[E8 extension page](e8_extension.md); the choices of uniform response in
2+1D have a separate [sector page](two_dimensional_sectors.md).

The model is mathematically explicit and reproducible. It is not yet a
universally identified physical classification of all insulators in its
intended sector. These are different claims, and the current evidence
supports them to different degrees.

**Definition and convention.**

The symmetry obeys `exp(i pi Q)=(-1)^F`. Write `G=G_f/U(1)^f`, with
`gQg^-1=(-1)^rho1(g) Q` and antiunitarity character `s1`. The action on the
charge circle is `rho1+s1`, not `rho1` alone. The extension is represented by
`omega2` with that coefficient action and by a specified real lift.

| Spacetime | CI decoration | Localized charge | Additive phase |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2+1D | `n0` in `C^0(G,Z_s1)` | `n2` in `C^2(G,Z_rho1)` | `nu3` in `C^3(G,R/Z_s1)` |
| 3+1D | `n1` in `C^1(G,Z_s1)` | `n3` in `C^3(G,Z_rho1)` | `nu4` in `C^4(G,R/Z_s1)` |
| 4+1D | `n2` in `C^2(G,Z_s1)` | `n4` in `C^4(G,Z_rho1)` | `nu5` in `C^5(G,R/Z_s1)` |

These are cochains subject to the dimensional equations. The table does not
declare every charge field closed. A complete state includes its phase
completion. Stacking and gauge reduction must retain that completion and
the phases generated by removing exact integer decorations. A raw
obstruction cochain rejects a state only when its class survives all
permitted completions and incoming identifications.

The adopted expressions come from the supplied v0.7 archive, with its fixed
sheet-linear calibration, cubic coefficient `1/6`, and additional product
coefficient `epsilon=0`. Their definition sites are the
[2+1D](../../formulas/03_two_dimensional.tex),
[3+1D](../../formulas/04_three_dimensional.tex), and
[4+1D](../../formulas/05_four_dimensional.tex) sections. The
[archive scope statement](../../formulas/11_reproducibility.tex) is part of
the specification, not a qualification removed by implementing the code.

**Evidence already available.**

The implementation uses exact integers and rationals and is checked against
the frozen supplied engines. Local source/product/gauge identities,
polynomial certificates, background changes, and complete root reductions
test the stated convention. The
[independent cyclic audit](../cyclic_root_audits.md) also verifies selected
relations without calling the GAP classification backend. Such checks can
establish that the program computes this model correctly; they cannot by
themselves construct an omitted physical realization map.

The relative 2+1D charge sector has the independent charge-conserving lattice
construction of Wang, Qi, Fang, Cheng, and Gu (2023),
[Exactly solvable lattice models for interacting electronic insulators in two dimensions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.15533).
For split `U(1)^f x C2`, the completed relative root has order four; its square
is the nontrivial bosonic phase. This test includes the phase completion and
the charge-removing gauge, rather than only a raw self-twister.

In 3+1D the archive verifies the following retained-model results:

| Background on `G=C2`, with character `alpha` | Complete-root result |
|---|---|
| AIII: `rho1=s1=alpha`, `omega2=0` | `Z8`, with `4X=(0,0,alpha^4/2)` |
| AII: `rho1=0`, `s1=alpha`, `omega2=alpha^2/2` | `Z2 x Z2`; the CI root squares to zero and the phase root survives |
| AI: `rho1=0`, `s1=alpha`, `omega2=0` | Trivial; the odd CI root is obstructed and the remaining phase is an incoming charge image |

These labels specify operator symmetries: AIII antiunitarity reverses
charge; AII and AI preserve charge and have squares `(-1)^F` and `1`,
respectively. They should not be identified by an antiunitary sign alone.

The AIII relation has a physical character check: Metlitski's eta-invariant
root obeys `exp(8 pi i eta)=(-1)^(integral w1^4)`, the response of eTmT.
See [Metlitski (2015), Eq. (3.45) and Section VI](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1510.05663).
This supports the distinguished fourth-power relation; it is not an
evaluation of every unsquared program state on every structured manifold.

**Physical identification that remains open.**

| Dimension | Required comparison still missing |
|---|---|
| 2+1D with nonzero uniform `n0` | A universal match of the full product to a specified physical P-move convention. The supplied product is an explicit consistent convention. |
| 3+1D | A universal terminal parity/physical phase map, compatible with product and all gauges, and reconciliation of the unresolved point-group comparisons. |
| 4+1D | The remaining universal comparison maps and an identification with the intended physical spectrum, including control of other response sectors. |

The nonzero-`n0` issue is spelled out in the source itself and is developed
on the [2D sector page](two_dimensional_sectors.md). Passing a polarization
identity cannot settle an arbitrary closed contribution to a physical
stacking correction.

Reference disagreements require checking their derivations as well as our
model. Arun Debray's
[2026 v3 bordism calculation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.02941v3), Table 5
and Theorem 4.82, prints `Z2^3` for the class-A spin-half proper groups
`D2` and `D6`, whereas the current campaign gives `Z2^2`. Our independent
[grading audit](debray_d2_grading_audit.md) finds a specific conflict
between the theorem's proof and its stated graded splitting. Recomputing
from that splitting and the independent Bruner--Mira--Stanley--Snaith
Theorem 2.12 gives `Omega_4 = Z^2 + Z2^2`; the free part of `Omega_5`
vanishes, so the Anderson-dual phase group is `Z2^2`. This corroborates
the model's group shape in these two cases, subject to the stated symmetry
dictionary. It is our mathematical audit, not a published erratum, and
the printed-reference fixture remains unchanged.

These two printed disagreements therefore do not establish a failure of
the retained model. They also do not supply the missing physical map or
identify its individual roots. No E8 factor is removed in this bordism
derivation; the bounded 3D argument on the
[E8 page](e8_extension.md) separately excludes an additional neutral-E8
`H^0` obstruction image for these finite proper groups.

**What a completed identification would provide.**

It should give a symmetry-preserving map from the retained cochain states
and their equivalences to physical partition functions or a specified
bordism/spectrum model. The map must preserve the CI, charge, and phase
filtration, send every allowed gauge to a physical equivalence, and
intertwine stacking, including its closed corrections. Its kernel and
image must then be determined. A group-order agreement alone supplies none
of these maps.

Until those steps are complete, “current CI/charge/phase model” is precise.
Calling it a subgroup of the full physical theory requires an injective
realization map; calling it a quotient by bosonic phases is a different
claim and is generally inappropriate, since the retained phase field
already includes bosonic responses.
