# From completed four-data roots to a stacking group

The [formula guide](../FORMULA_GUIDE.md) fixes the state order
`(e_p,n_p,n_{p+2},nu_{p+3})`. A filtration quotient records possible
leading decorations. The final group also records what their completed
powers become in the lower layers.

## Complete the roots and quotient incoming images

First compute signed integer sheet classes, retaining both free and torsion
generators. Solve the complete integer obstruction for charge. Test the
terminal obstruction only modulo the permitted charge adjustments and
incoming images. Complete each surviving root with an actual phase
primitive. Save that primitive and all changes of lower representatives.

Closed sheet and charge gauge parameters can generate nonzero lower
states. Include their complete images as relations, with their phases.
A closed parameter is not automatically an identity cochain. When a
kernel is computed over integers, use a saturated integer lattice and
then impose the original torsion relations; a rational nullspace or an
unsaturated set of multiples can change the classification.

For free sheets, choose completed generators of the full surviving
lattice. A fixed nonzero leading-coordinate fiber is a torsor, not a
stacking subgroup. Lower obstructions of combinations must be evaluated
using the full product, including the integer and phase twisters.

## Calculate every relation with the chosen gauges

Let `X_i` be a completed root and let `m_i` be its order in its filtration
quotient. Form `X_i^(m_i)` using the full stacking law. Remove its exact
leading coordinate with an actual gauge primitive. The resulting charge
and phase can be nonzero. Express them in completed lower roots, subtract
those roots with their full inverses, and repeat until only an exact phase
remains. Save the final phase primitive as a witness.

This yields a relation

```math
m_iX_i-\sum_{j>i}a_{ij}X_j=0
```

in an E8-first presentation. Include incoming relations and relations
among lower generators as well. A raw diagonal twister, or the integer
order of the leading cochain, does not determine `m_i X_i` after gauge
removal. Short charge gauges and full source-cylinder gauges need not be
equal pointwise; retain the phase of the gauge actually used.

Smith form of the complete integer relation matrix gives finite invariant
factors and free rank. Preserve the relation matrix and its witnesses,
not only the Smith factors. To recover a filtration layer from this
matrix, intersect the relation lattice with the subgroup generated by
that layer and all lower ones before taking the quotient. Reading
diagonal entries without this elimination is not valid in general.

## The shared phase couples the two sheet rows

There is one phase coordinate. In 4+1D, a neutral relation can be
`3X=b` while a CI relation also contains `b`. Taking separate classifications
for the E8 and CI sectors and multiplying them would duplicate this
generator and lose the coupled relation. The
[cyclic examples](FOUR_DIMENSIONAL_CYCLIC_EXAMPLES.md) compute this shared
presentation explicitly.

In the selected 3+1D section, the neutral source, product, and gauge have
zero additional phase. There the section supplies an independent
`H1(G,Z_s)` presentation. The conclusion follows from those specified
operations; it is not inferred from the existence of two sheet rows.

The resulting group is the classification of the adopted cochain model.
An identification with the full physical theory additionally needs the
physical comparison stated on each dimensional formula page.
