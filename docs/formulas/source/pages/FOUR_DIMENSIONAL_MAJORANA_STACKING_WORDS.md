cochain operations and remain structured arguments of each MS term.

arguments when they occur. Reuse preserves the exact lower input and
coefficient convention. Inserting a lower formula is optional. The symbol
$`d`$ is the cochain differential, not an AHSS map $`d_r`$. Each specified
