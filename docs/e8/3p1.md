# 3+1D: neutral cocycles

The ordered fields are `(e1,n1,n3,nu4)`.

## Obstructions

\[
d_s e_1=d_s n_1=0,\qquad d_\rho n_3=-B_3\cup n_1,\qquad
d_s\nu_4=\widehat O_5^{\rm CI}(n_1,n_3).
\]

Here `B3=d_(rho+s)(omega_real-s1^2/2)` in the retained convention.
The CI source is defined in `formulas/04_three_dimensional.tex`.
The neutral cocycle is integral; binary reduction loses weak layers.

## Stacking and gauge

In the chosen neutral section, `e1` adds and transforms by `e1+d_s v0`.
The existing CI charge and common-phase product/gauge remain in place.
Zero additional group-cochain phase does not mean zero gravitational response.

`insulator.e8_h1.twisted_h1` computes `H1(G,Z_s)` from a finite presentation,
including infinite groups. It retains a saturated integer cocycle kernel,
the principal-coboundary relation, free rank, and torsion. It does not
truncate translations to a finite cyclic group.

For ordinary three-dimensional space groups and their orientation character,
the physical neutral section is supported by Song, Xiong, and Huang,
[PRB 101, 165129 (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.101.165129).
Electronic survival additionally uses coefficient inclusion, AHSS naturality,
and absence of incoming maps from negative filtration. This justifies a
selected split presentation where those physical hypotheses apply, rather
than imposing vanishing mixed terms in every coordinate system.

This does not identify the retained CI algorithm with the entire physical
kernel. For infinite groups, compute `H1`; do not use the finite-group
shortcut `0` or `Z2`.
