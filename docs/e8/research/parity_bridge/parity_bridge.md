## 1. Reorganizing the same symmetry is different from forgetting symmetry

Let $G=G_f/U(1)^f$ and $G_b=G_f/\mathbb Z_2^f$. Set $\chi=\rho_1+s_1$. Charge conjugation is measured by $\rho_1$; conjugation of complex scalars is measured by $s_1$. Their sum, not either character alone, acts on the circle.

Choose a normalized section of $G_f\to G$ and a real extension lift $\omega_2^{\mathbb R}$. In circle coordinates of period one, the bosonic quotient has multiplication

$$(x,g)(y,h)=([x+(-1)^{\chi(g)}y+2\omega_2^{\mathbb R}(g,h)]_1,gh).$$

For $X_1(x,g)=x$, define

$$M_2=d_\chi X_1+2\pi^*\omega_2^{\mathbb R},\qquad \omega_2^{\rm SC}=M_2\bmod2.$$

The product law makes $M_2$ integer. Its derivative is $2\pi^*d_\chi\omega_2^{\mathbb R}$, hence its mod-two residue is a cocycle. Lifting $(x,g)$ to $(x/2,g)$ in $G_f$ produces precisely the parity defect $(-1)^{M_2}$. This proves the extension dictionary at the group level. With the checked convention,

$$\check M_2=M_2-(s_1^2)_\chi,\quad d_\chi\check M_2=2\pi^*B_3,$$
$$\beta_\chi\check\omega_2^{\rm SC}=\pi^*B_3-d_\chi\lfloor\check M_2/2\rfloor.$$

These are exact section/carry identities for the discrete quotient; no finite-group averaging enters. Their source is the supplied v0.7 note, `note/sections/07_unified.tex:31–59`, Eqs. `circle-law` through `circle-bockstein`. The fibration is $BU(1)_b\to BG_b\to BG$. Ordinary cochains on a discretized charge circle would solve a different problem.

Keeping the full $G_f$ and using its two quotients is a change of organization of the same tangential symmetry type. At the spectrum/structured-manifold level it should not change the classification, provided both descriptions implement that same full theory. It need not preserve the two AHSS filtrations. By contrast, actually breaking $U(1)$ is restriction along a specified parity-containing subgroup $H_f\hookrightarrow G_f$. There need not be a preferred lift of the residual quotient $G$ to such a subgroup for a nonsplit extension. Thus there is no universal instruction “delete $U(1)$ and keep the same $G,\omega$.”

## 3. A complete exact bridge for the CF-only sector

This supplies source, product, and gauge, rather than source agreement alone. Set $e_p=n_p=0$, write $z=n_{p+2}\in Z^{p+2}(G,\mathbb Z_{\rho_1})$, and put $k=p+2=d$. Pullbacks to $G_b$ are implicit. In the short CF coordinates use

$$x=\bar z,\qquad \nu_{k+1}^{\rm SC}=\nu_{k+1}+R_{k+1}(z),\qquad R_{k+1}(z)=\tfrac12X_1\cup z.$$

The sources obey

$$O^{\rm SC}_{k+2}(\bar z)-O^{\rm CI}_{k+2}(z)=d_sR_{k+1}(z).$$

Indeed $d_s(X_1z/2)=M_2z/2-\omega_2^{\mathbb R}z-X_1d_\rho z/2$ and $M_2z/2\equiv\omega_2^{\rm SC}\bar z/2$. Both short products use the same open polarization $F(z,z')$, so $E^{\rm CI}=E^{\rm SC}$ and $\Delta R=0$: the product primitive is $K=0$.

For an integral gauge $u\in C^{k-1}(G,\mathbb Z_\rho)$, map its parity parameter to $\bar u$. With the short gauges

$$G^{\rm CI}(z;u)=\omega_2^{\mathbb R}u+\tfrac12\mathrm{Sq}^2\bar u+F(z,d_\rho u),$$
$$G^{\rm SC}(\bar z;\bar u)=\tfrac12(\omega_2^{\rm SC}\bar u+\mathrm{Sq}^2\bar u)+F(\bar z,d\bar u),$$

the full endpoint comparison is

$$G^{\rm CI}+R(z+d_\rho u)-R(z)-G^{\rm SC}=d_s(-X_1u/2).$$

The same calculation on an **open** charge gives the required extra term

$$O^{\rm SC}(\bar z)-O^{\rm CI}(z)-d_sR(z)=X_1d_\rho z/2.$$

It cannot be discarded when the CI sheet opens the charge equation. For the shifted input $\bar z+J$, the additional terms are exactly v0.7 Eq. `open-charge-subtraction`, `07_unified.tex:132–160`. The proof uses only signed Leibniz and the open-square polarization and applies in all three dimensions and to the stated infinite groups. `check_charge_bridge.py` passed 96 exact checks with independent $s,\rho$, negative integers, non-half-valued real lifts, and both closed/open charges. These checks use the stated short gauges; translating a different native/cylinder representative requires its phase rephasing.

## 5. What remains for a universal nonzero-CI comparison

Let $f$ be the complete lower map, including carries, and let $a,b$ denote E8-first states. A terminal comparison still requires specified universal cochains satisfying

$$d_sR_{p+3}(a)=O^{\rm SC}_{p+4}(f(a))-\pi^*(O^{\rm CI}_{p+4}(a)+O^{E8}_{p+4}(a)),$$
$$d_sK_{p+2}(a,b)=\pi^*E_{p+3}(a,b)+\Delta R_{p+3}(a,b)
-E^{\rm SC}_{p+3}(f(a),f(b))-G^{\rm SC}_{p+3}(\lambda(a,b)).$$

Here $\lambda$ is the oriented lower path from the product of mapped inputs to the mapped product. The charge-dependent part of $R$ is explicit above and in v0.7 §7. The remaining CI/background/circle-carry part, and the paired two-input primitive, have not been constructed by this audit. The newly proved pure-neutral product restriction does not supply them. In particular, the v0.7 source-invisible two-input operation $\rho_1\bar n_2\bar n'_2/2$ (`07_unified.tex:162–176`) explains why the first equality alone cannot prove the second.

A homotopy can construct these maps **after** the relevant universal residual is shown null in the chosen natural cochain complex. A primitive on each contractible simplex is insufficient: it may fail naturality, normalization, or descent to group cochains. Finite-group period checks and calibration on a finite set of backgrounds do not by themselves prove universal nullity. A complete method is to compare the two Postnikov presentations of the same topological symmetry spectrum, retaining the charge-circle skeleton through degree six, and transfer the source, products, and gauges together. Alternatively one can solve the explicit universal residual and its product obstruction in a justified finite coefficient basis. Both routes must preserve the fixed CI normalization and record any nonzero closed remainder rather than declare it exact.

There is a further necessary enlargement for intrinsic responses: the simple lower map $2n_p+16e_p$ describes the retained decoration tower, not the full coefficient row. See `intrinsic_response.md` for an explicit $G=1$ example showing why circle-dependent intrinsic data must be included.
