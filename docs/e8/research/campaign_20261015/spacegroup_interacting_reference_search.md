## Translation-only control for P1

Charles Zhaoxi Xiong and A. Alexandradinata, *Organizing symmetry-protected topological phases by layering and symmetry reduction: a minimalist perspective*, [arXiv:1709.06998v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.06998v3) (initial 2017-09-20; v3 2018-03-04), [Physical Review B 97, 115153 (2018)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.97.115153), Sec. VIII.B, proves the split translation relation

`A_d(Z × G) = A_d(G) ⊕ A_(d−1)(G)`

under the paper's **Twisted Generalized Cohomology Hypothesis**. The method is generalized cohomology with a physical layer-construction interpretation. See cached source lines 1403–1423, proposition `prp:strong_weak`, equations `SES_translation` and `strong_weak`; the hypothesis is stated at lines 913–923.

Daniel S. Freed and Michael J. Hopkins, *Reflection positivity and invertible topological phases*, [arXiv:1604.06527v6](https://arxiv.org/abs/1604.06527v6) (initial 2016-04-22; v6 2022-12-18), [Geometry & Topology 25, 1165–1330 (2021)](https://doi.org/10.2140/gt.2021.25.1165), gives interacting reflection-positive Spin^c theory coefficients. Use the **TP column**, not the free-fermion column, of the symmetry-class-A corollary `thm:95`, equation `eq:115`, cached lines 6596–6613. In spatial dimensions 0,1,2,3 these are `Z, 0, Z², 0`. Its physical application assumes the invertible low-energy field-theory description.

Iterating the split translation relation three times gives, independently of the classifier,

`A_3(Z³ × U(1)^f) = A_3 ⊕ 3 A_2 ⊕ 3 A_1 ⊕ A_0 = Z^7`.

The generators can be counted as three neutral E8 layers, three IQH layers, and one integer filling. This retains atomic charge/filling and neutral E8; removing either changes the stated scope. P1 has no nonidentity point operation, hence no distinction between the two catalogue spin lifts. This is a derivation from primary coefficient and splitting statements, **not a printed seven-generator P1 classification table**. It supplies a useful conditional physical control but does not remove the physical-spectrum assumption for all other space groups.

## Single-glide interacting control

Fuyan Lu, Bowen Shi and Yuan-Ming Lu, *Classification and surface anomaly of glide symmetry protected topological phases in three dimensions*, [arXiv:1701.00784v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.00784v2) (initial 2017-01-03; v2 2017-01-22), [New Journal of Physics 19, 073002 (2017)](https://doi.org/10.1088/1367-2630/aa769b), uses coupled-layer dimensional reduction and anomalous interacting surface topological orders. Table II, class A, and Sec. IV.D identify two order-two roots, neutral E8 and IQH. The symmetry is generated by one glide and U(1), and the paper classifies states trivial after glide is forgotten. Cached source lines 156–168, 223, 887–900 and 1000–1017 specify the group, scope and roots. The charge convention is fundamental fermion charge e, with `c−=8ν+q` and `σxy=q e²/h`.

Xiong–Alexandradinata, Sec. VI.A, cached lines 1252–1262, upgrades this to the complete interacting **single-glide** class-A group `Z2 × Z2`, using their exact sequence and the absence of a strong 3D U(1)-only phase. This remains a rank-one nonsymmorphic subgroup result. SG14 also has inversion/screw structure and three independent translations, so the single-glide answer cannot be copied into its full-space-group cell. A restriction homomorphism and compatible root backgrounds would be required for a precise sector comparison. The paper does not provide our two full crystalline spin-lift tables.

## Filling constraints, not stacking classifications

Haruki Watanabe, Hoi Chun Po, Ashvin Vishwanath and Michael P. Zaletel, *Filling constraints for spin-orbit coupled insulators in symmorphic and non-symmorphic crystals*, [arXiv:1505.04193v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.04193v4) (initial 2015-05-15; v4 2016-03-17), [PNAS 112, 14551 (2015)](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1514665112), uses entanglement arguments and compact flat Bieberbach manifolds. Its theorem explicitly assumes physical Kramers time reversal as well as U(1) and the space group (cached lines 43–65). Table I gives SG4 filling `4n` and SG19 `8n`; Appendix table `Bbb1` also gives SG14 `4n` and symmorphic groups `2n` (lines 622–635). These are interacting symmetric short-range-entangled **filling** constraints, not classification groups. Our antiunitary crystalline-equivalence character is not an additional physical Kramers time reversal, so these numerical bounds must not be imposed on the present inputs.

S. A. Parameswaran, Ari M. Turner, Daniel P. Arovas and Ashvin Vishwanath, *Topological Order and Absence of Band Insulators at Integer Filling in Non-Symmorphic Crystals*, [arXiv:1212.0557v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1212.0557v2) (initial 2012-12-03; v2 2012-12-10), [Nature Physics 9, 299–303 (2013)](https://doi.org/10.1038/nphys2600), proves nonperturbative flux-threading filling constraints. It covers spinless particles and spin-conserving electrons; arbitrary spin-orbit coupling is explicitly deferred (cached lines 168, 191, 204). An essential twofold screw or glide forces even filling, by the commutation relation `eq:FCR` at lines 226–233. Thus SG4, SG14 and SG19 have a useful spinless even-filling necessary condition. This does not prove sharpness for SG19, nor classify roots, torsion or stacking extensions. The condition must not be copied as a general spin-half bound without checking the spin-action hypotheses.

## Consequences for the selected inputs

- SG1 P1: the conditional `Z^7` translation derivation above is available, with atomic filling and neutral E8 retained.
- SG2 P−1: no matching full electronic table was found; an inversion-only point-group result omits translations and their mixed invariants.
- SG4 P2_1: a spinless necessary even-filling condition is available; the Kramers-time-reversal `4n` result has different symmetry.
- SG6 Pm: a mirror-only interacting classification does not include its three translations and should not be treated as a full-group result.
- SG14 P2_1/c: the single-glide sector and filling constraints are useful leads, but neither is a full affine classification.
- SG19 P2_12_12_1: the spinless even-filling necessary condition is available, while the cited `8n` Bieberbach result assumes Kramers time reversal. An abstract computed `Z` alone says nothing about the primitive-cell filling of its generator; use the actual filling witness.

The full electronic reference cells therefore remain UNVERIFIED. Song–Xiong–Huang's separately audited 230-group comparison remains strictly the neutral E8 coefficient sector.
